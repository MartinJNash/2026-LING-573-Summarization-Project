Imagine you are a human annotator. Evaluate the quality of a generated plain language summary of a clinical document on the following criteria:

1. **Informativeness**: Does it cover key findings, diagnosis, and treatment plan without hallucinating?
2. **Simplification**: Does it use simple vocabulary and minimal jargon? Is it accessible to a lay reader?
3. **Faithfulness**: Is it factually aligned with its source (no substitutions or errors)?

Scores range from 0 (worst) to 100 (best).

Output ONLY a JSON object of this format: {"informativeness": <info_score>, "simplification": <simp_score>, "faithfulness": <fait_score>}
Do not include any explanations, preamble, or other text/data.
